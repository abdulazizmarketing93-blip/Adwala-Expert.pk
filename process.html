How We Work

Hamare proven step-by-step process se samjhein ke hum kaise aapke business ke liye results deliver karte hain.

01
Discovery Phase

Free Consultation & Business Analysis

Pehla step hai aapko samajhna. Hum aapke business, goals, target audience, competitors, aur current digital presence ko deeply analyze karte hain. Ye step bilkul free hai aur koi commitment nahi.

Is phase mein hum aapke saath ek detailed meeting karte hain (WhatsApp ya Zoom pe), aapki existing marketing dekhte hain, aur identify karte hain ke kahan improvement ki zaroorat hai.

Free Consultation Business Goals Discussion Competitor Analysis Current Audit
02
Strategy Phase

Custom Strategy & Roadmap Creation

Analysis ke baad hum aapke liye ek custom digital marketing strategy banate hain. Ye strategy aapke specific business goals, budget, aur timeline ke mutabiq hoti hai — koi copy-paste approach nahi.

Strategy mein clearly define karte hain: kya channels use honge, kya milega, kitne time mein, aur expected ROI kya hoga.

Custom Strategy KPI Definition Timeline Planning Budget Allocation
03
Setup Phase

Technical Setup & Campaign Creation

Strategy approve hone ke baad hamaari team technical setup shuru karti hai. Ad accounts, pixels, tracking codes, landing pages — sab kuch properly configure hota hai pehle hi campaign launch se.

Ye phase ensure karta hai ke sab data correctly track ho aur koi budget waste na ho setup issues ki wajah se.

Pixel & Tracking Setup Ad Account Configuration Creative Production Landing Page Optimization
04
Launch Phase

Campaign Launch & Initial Testing

Campaign live hoti hai strategically — hum chhote budget se shuru karte hain, multiple ad variations test karte hain, aur data collect karte hain best-performing elements find karne ke liye.

Pehle 2 weeks mein hum closely monitor karte hain aur quick adjustments karte hain based on initial data.

A/B Testing Performance Monitoring Real-time Optimization Daily Check-ins
05
Optimization Phase

Data-Driven Optimization & Scaling

Jab data aata hai, hum best-performing ads aur audiences ko scale karte hain aur under-performing elements ko pause ya improve karte hain. Ye continuous cycle hai jab tak maximum ROI achieve na ho jaaye.

Hamaari team weekly basis pe campaigns optimize karti hai — keywords, audiences, bids, creatives — sab kuch tune kiya jaata hai.

Budget Optimization Audience Refinement Creative Iteration ROI Maximization
06
Reporting Phase

Transparent Reporting & Long-term Partnership

Hum aapko monthly detailed performance reports dete hain — sab kuch clearly explain karte hain: kitna spend hua, kitna result mila, aur agle month ka plan kya hai. Koi confusing jargon nahi, sab simple aur clear.

Hamaara goal ek long-term partnership hai — aapki business growth ke saath hamaari services bhi grow karti hain. Hum aapke dedicated digital marketing partner hain.

Monthly Reports Strategy Review Meetings Growth Planning Long-term Partnership
